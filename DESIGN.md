## Brand & Style

The design system projects architectural permanence, bespoke luxury, and editorial discretion. Tailored for ultra-high-net-worth individuals, institutional investors, and connoisseurs of prime real estate, the emotional signature is serene, commanding, and impeccably curated.

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Editorial Luxury** with **Purity of Structure**:
- Monolithic structures balanced by deliberate whitespace and generous breathing room.
- Rejection of ephemeral tech trends (no excessive neon glows, no synthetic claymorphism).
- High visual discipline reminiscent of prestigious architectural monographs, gallery archives, and bespoke horology maisons.
- Subtle golden bronze details used strictly as micro-accents to anchor precision and value without ostentation.

## Colors

The palette banishes all organic greenery or generic corporate blues in favor of pure architectural geology: deep carbon, warm stone, and polished golden bronze.

### Roles & Application
- **Deep Architectural Obsidian (`#0A0A0A` & `#111111`)**: The foundation. Serves as the canvas backdrop, dark surface containers, and prime grounding layers.
- **Architectural Charcoal (`#222222`)**: Intermediate surfaces, subtle dividers, line rules, and secondary component tiles.
- **Golden Bronze (`#C8A86B` / `#B89758`)**: Trevita’s signature metal. Used with restraint for focus rings, premium badges, selected active states, fine hairpins, and bespoke numerical displays. Never used for massive flooded backdrops.
- **Warm Alabaster & Ivory White (`#FFFFFF`, `#FAFAF7`)**: Pure editorial text, high-contrast headings, and crisp visual callouts.
- **Champagne Stone & Warm Beige (`#EFECE6`, `#D8D2C5`)**: Secondary typography, captioning, architectural spec labels, and light-mode contrasting paper cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the classical proportions of serif headlines (`EB Garamond`) with the structural geometry of `Manrope` for body, technical data, and micro-labels.

### Implementation Guidelines
- **Headings (EB Garamond)**: Set in regular to medium weights. Rendered in `#FAFAF7` over obsidian or `#111111` over stone. Avoid artificial bolding; dignity derives from size and proportional balance.
- **Body Text (Manrope)**: Set predominantly in light (`300`) and regular (`400`) weights with elevated line heights (`1.6x` - `1.8x`) to maximize readability in high-contrast editorial passages.
- **Labels & Metrology (Manrope Uppercase)**: All property specifications (e.g., `SQ. FT.`, `FLOOR PLAN`, `PENTHOUSE RESIDENCE`) are tracked wide (`0.15em` to `0.2em`), capitalized, and tinted in bronze (`#C8A86B`) or stone beige (`#D8D2C5`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors modern architectural blueprints: disciplined, expansive, and aligned to a strict 12-column grid.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Single-column focus flow with generous top/bottom padding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, max container width `1440px` (or `1600px` for wide displays), `margin: 4rem`, `gutter: 2.5rem`.

### Rhythmic Philosophy
Whitespace is a primary luxury material. Sections are separated by sweeping vertical distances (`space-xl` and above) rather than heavy dividers. Content blocks must never crowd; negative space frames images and floorplans like art pieces in a private pavilion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects blurry dropshadows and exaggerated 3D skeuomorphism. Instead, the design system implements **Architectural Tonal Stratification and Micro-Hairlines**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas)**: Deep Architectural Obsidian (`#0A0A0A`).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Foundation)**: Solid Architectural Black (`#111111`) or subtle dark slate (`#161616`).
- **Layer 2 (Overlays & Flying Drawers)**: Pure Carbon (`#1A1A1A`) surfaced with an ultra-thin 1px hairline border of `rgba(200, 168, 107, 0.25)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Micro-Shadows**: When elevation is structurally unavoidable (e.g., hovering navigation bar or property detail modal), apply an imperceptible, highly diffused tinted ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Sharp (`0`)** shape language.

- All containers, media viewports, property gallery tiles, input frames, and buttons feature pristine, razor-sharp `0px` corners.
- Sharp geometry conveys permanence, structural solidity, and timeless architectural integrity.
- Micro-elements (such as tooltips, active tab pips, or small badge indicators) remain strictly rectilinear.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary Action**: Sharp rectilinear outline in Trevita Golden Bronze (`#C8A86B`) or solid Golden Bronze fill with black text (`#0A0A0A`) in uppercase `Manrope 600` with `0.15em` letter spacing. Padding: `1rem 2.5rem`.
- **Secondary Action**: Monolithic border (`1px solid #333333`) over dark background (`#111111`), transitioning on hover to `border-color: #C8A86B` and text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text / Editorial Action**: Raw text link with an ultra-fine 1px bottom bronze accent line offset by `6px`, extending smoothly across width upon hover.

### Cards (Residences & Penthouses)
- **Structure**: Razor-sharp image frames with 4:3 or 16:9 ratios. Deep black `#111111` backing.
- **Accents**: 1px perimeter border in `#222222`. On hover, the border subtly illuminates to `#C8A86B` (at 40% opacity) alongside a gentle `1.02x` cinematic zoom on the architectural photography.
- **Metadata**: Property specifications (Beds, Baths, Area) displayed in champagne stone (`#D8D2C5`) separated by hairline vertical bronze dividers (`#B89758`).

### Input Fields & Inquiries
- Underlined or framed in 1px architectural charcoal (`#2A2A2A`).
- Background: completely transparent or `#141414`.
- Text: Warm Alabaster (`#FAFAF7`). Floating labels in uppercase Manrope (`label-sm`).
- Focus State: Border transitions to crisp Trevita Bronze (`#C8A86B`) without outer glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom 16px rectilinear boxes with 1px border (`#444444`).
- Selected State: Inner filled square (for checkboxes) or solid diamond/square indicator in `#C8A86B`. No curved radiuses.

### Chips & Floorplan Switchers
- Inactive: Sharp borders (`1px solid #222222`), transparent background, text in `#888888`.
- Active: Bordered in `#C8A86B`, text in `#FAFAF7`, background `#1A1712`.

### Architectural Spec Tables
- Tabular data bordered top and bottom with crisp 1px `#222222` rules.
- Labels in `#D8D2C5` (uppercase), numerical values right-aligned in `#FAFAF7` with serif numerals or precision geometric glyphs.
